## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Cancellation Rate by Hotel

Compares cancellation rates between **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel** to identify which hotel type experiences higher cancellation activity.

### 2. Cancellation Rate by Month

Analyzes monthly booking cancellation patterns to identify seasonal trends and periods with higher cancellation risk.

### 3. Cancellation Rate by Lead Time

Shows the relationship between booking lead time and cancellation rate, helping identify booking periods associated with higher cancellation risk.

### 4. Cancellation Rate by Market Segment

Analyzes cancellation behavior across different market segments such as Groups, Online TA, Offline TA/TO, Aviation, Corporate, Direct, and Complementary.

### 5. Cancellation Rate by Deposit Type

Compares cancellation behavior across **Non Refund, No Deposit, and Refundable** booking types.

### 6. Estimated Revenue by Hotel

Visualizes estimated revenue contribution from **City Hotel** and **Resort Hotel**.

### 7. Customer Type Analysis

Analyzes cancellation distribution across **Transient, Contract, Transient-Party, and Group** customers.

### 8. Risk Level Analysis

Categorizes bookings into **Low Risk, Medium Risk, and High Risk** to identify the overall cancellation-risk distribution.

## 💡 Key Insights

* The dashboard contains approximately **119.39K total bookings**.
* Around **44K bookings were cancelled**, resulting in a cancellation rate of approximately **37%**.
* Estimated revenue is **42.72M**, with approximately **16.73M estimated revenue lost** due to cancellations.
* **Non-refundable bookings** show significantly higher cancellation activity.
* Cancellation patterns vary across **market segments and customer types**.
* Lead-time analysis helps identify booking windows associated with greater cancellation risk.
* Risk-level analysis provides a quick overview of **low-, medium-, and high-risk bookings**.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
* **Excel / CSV** – Data source and preprocessing

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform hotel booking data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that helps businesses:

* Monitor booking and cancellation performance
* Identify high-risk bookings
* Analyze cancellation trends
* Measure revenue loss caused by cancellations
* Understand customer and market-segment behavior
* Compare hotel performance
* Support data-driven revenue management decisions

## 📌 Dashboard Features

* Interactive slicers for **Hotel, Arrival Year, Arrival Month, Distribution Channel, Deposit Type, Market Segment, Customer Type, and Risk Level**
* KPI cards for quick performance monitoring
* Monthly cancellation trend analysis
* Hotel-wise cancellation comparison
* Market segment analysis
* Deposit type analysis
* Customer type analysis
* Revenue and revenue-loss analysis
* Risk-level distribution
